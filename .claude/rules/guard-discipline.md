# Guard discipline

Keep all nine generic objectives. Show actual positive and negative invocation, not merely a script or hook existing. Prove the legal next task succeeds after the predecessor, including a changed actual execution and owned worktree; also test old-card misuse, unpublished/changed plans, wrong scope/role/purpose, same-execution review, stale evidence and business refusal. Deliberately remove each critical call in an isolated fixture and prove the refusal test detects it; restore exact bytes.

Source tools stay byte-identical to the pinned audited blobs. Current control reports bind current implementations. Preserve historical BA001 reports as historical evidence; fresh GOV002 reports live separately. Synthetic fixtures and legacy compatibility tests do not establish product acceptance or a real GitHub task transition.

Inspect every public-tree file, not only the diff. Reject secrets/private source IDs, symlinks, invalid text and runtime/binary payloads. Do not copy old product code/tests or probe old databases/backups. New foundation work gets independently written build/inert-host/isolated-data checks; an empty suite or a passing echo is not acceptance.
