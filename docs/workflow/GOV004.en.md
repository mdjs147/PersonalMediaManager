- Bind the exact old SHA and candidate. Every public summary points to one exact private evidence set; replacement, trimming, wrong candidates, stale records and missing originals reject
- Private CI runs a trusted fixed verifier and treats the candidate as data. A job with private originals or credentials cannot execute candidate scripts or load candidate plugins. Product execution occurs separately with no private data or credentials, and a trusted collector binds the results
- Private CI reports success only after the complete required checks. Missing, failed, cancelled, timed-out, wrong-issuer/version/digest and superseded failed outcomes block the public gate
- A GitHub status is an authenticated assertion from the private reporting account. Public CI must not call it its own independent reading of the private originals or proof of remote branch protection
- Public summaries, logs, job names, status descriptions and errors receive allowlist-based content review. Malicious evidence text cannot be interpolated into a shell or copied into public output
- Independent design/implementation review, original positive/negative evidence, affected current-candidate tests, whole-public-tree semantic review and exact stage CI must all complete
